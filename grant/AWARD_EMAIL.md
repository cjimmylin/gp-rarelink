Hi there,

I'm excited to share that your project has been selected for Anthropic's AI for Science [rare disease research program](https://www.anthropic.com/news/rare-disease-research-grants)! Here is a bit about the cohort you're joining, what your project was approved for, what I need from you, and a few things coming your way.

**Two notes before you start**

* Our program rules have changed. Under the updated [AI for Science program rules](https://www.anthropic.com/ai-for-science-program-rules), whether your conversations are used for model training always follows the privacy settings on your own Claude account. The program doesn't change them, so please review [Settings > Privacy](https://claude.ai/settings/data-privacy-controls) on the account you'll use and set it the way you want before you start.
* If you're working with clinical or patient data, please make sure everything you do with Claude stays within your data use agreements, IRB or ethics approvals. You're responsible for the data you bring into your project, so please be thoughtful about what you upload and share.

**About the cohort**

* We received more than 750 applications for this program! The final cohort consists of 100 projects covering more than 75 named rare conditions.
* Many projects across the program are tackling similar challenges and ideas, including mechanism discovery and cross-disease mapping, phenotype extraction and natural-history curation, variant interpretation, therapeutic design (ASOs, gene therapy, protein and small-molecule work) and trial readiness, and benchmarking of AI reasoning on rare disease problems.

**Your award**

* Your project is approved for the amount you requested in your application, up to the program maximum of $50,000, as extra usage credits for Claude over a six-month project period. These are deposited to a [claude.ai](https://claude.ai) account and work across everything there: chat, Claude Code, Cowork, and Claude Science.
* We send credits on a rolling basis: I'll deposit the first half up front and top things up toward the approved total as you spend down. Credits expire six months after deposit.

**What I need from you by September 14**

* The Organization ID for the [claude.ai](https://claude.ai) account you'd like the credits sent to. Log in at [claude.ai](https://claude.ai), go to [Settings > Account](https://claude.ai/settings/account), and copy the Organization ID listed there. Please reply to this email with the ID itself rather than the account email, and confirm it even if you included one in your application.
* It needs to be a paid account (Pro or Max, or a Team plan that your lab or company owns). Claude Science only runs on paid plans. If you don't currently have a paid account, just reply and let me know and I'll help figure it out.
* Please use a personal, lab or company account rather than an institutional Enterprise account. We can't deposit credits into accounts that sit under a university or hospital enterprise license right now, so if that's how you usually access Claude, send the ID for a personal or lab-owned account instead.
* If several people on your project need credits, there are two options, since a personal Pro or Max account can't add members. Either use a Team plan that your lab or company owns and send me that Organization ID, so everyone on the team draws from the same credits, or send me the Organization ID for each person's account along with how you'd like the award split between them.
* Turn on usage credits on that account. Go to [Settings > Usage](https://claude.ai/settings/usage), find Usage credits, and click Enable. It may ask for a payment method; nothing is charged for this. Set the monthly limit to whatever you're comfortable with, keeping your award in mind. Here is a [help article on usage credits](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/12429409-manage-usage-credits-for-paid-claude-plans) for reference.

I'll send the first deposit on September 21. Credits typically show up in the account within a few business days.
