## What was checked, and how

- **Tables (script).** Every numeric token in every `tabular`/`tabularx` row (29 table environments, 306 rows, 2,694 numeric tokens) was matched against the log. Every token occurs in the log. A row-by-row match (paper row against the best single log line) left 67 rows that combine two log tables or reorder columns: the registered-six summary (log Table 4 + Table 6), the records table (Table 6 + the disease-term column of §2.0), the O2 classification table (Table 9 + the D-19 table), the extension repeat + O2 table (two log tables, with *Condition names disease* and *HPO annotation recall* swapped), the open-weight table (§2.1.10, two tables), the addendum tables (batch-number column added) and the pending table. All 67 were then checked by hand, cell by cell, including that the swapped extension columns carry the right values. **No wrong number in any table.**
- **Figures.** The hard-coded data in `plots/*/plot.py` (gatekeeper by run, O2 concordance, access/OMIM) match the log, including every interval. Captions checked by hand.
- **Main text (pages 1–10) and appendix prose.** Read sentence by sentence against the log: about 990 numeric tokens. No number differs from the log in quantity, condition, denominator or date.
- **Facts from outside the log** (Wakap 2019 3.5–5.9%; Faye 2024 "close to five years"; Lyons 2023 24% in SLC6A8; Jensen 2025 94.1% → 77.2%; Grimm 2015) were checked against the abstracts in `lit/registry.json` / `lit/citation_map.json`: all supported. Facts from the project's own references match their descriptions in `notes.md`.
- **Pending results.** 29 distinct `\HR{}` ids are used, all on the list in `pending_human_results.md`, and none is missing. `latex/human_results.tex` defines no value (as of 2026-11-20). No value or range is stated for a pending result. One direction is implied (finding 1).
- **Citations.** `po.py check` (run on a copy in `latex/`, then deleted): 87 cited keys, **0 unknown keys**, no missing figures, balanced environments.
- **Rules.** Checked for names other than the two authors, rater identity, the capped kind or the cap's size, verbatim quotes, the SLIDERS ablation, the uncorrected grant figures, registered/extension/Majeed/addendum separation, and 214 vs 269. The cap is not named and no size is given. There is no verbatim quote, no ablation, and every grant figure appears only in its corrected form.
