## Form title

GP-RareLink: tell us who you are and how you would like to contribute

## Description (shown under the title)

Thank you for looking at GP-RareLink. This short form asks who you are, which parts of the project you could help with, and what you make of the interim results. It takes about five minutes. We will use your answers only to organize the collaboration; we will not share them. Everything in the project is published literature; there is no patient data.

## Questions

1. **Name** (short answer, required)
2. **Email** (short answer, required; "Collect email addresses" can also be switched on in the form settings)
3. **Affiliation** (short answer)
4. **Your background** (checkboxes, pick all that apply)
   - Clinical genetics or genetic counselling
   - Rare-disease clinician
   - Variant curation or interpretation (ClinVar, ACMG/AMP)
   - Bioinformatics or natural-language processing
   - Phenotyping / HPO
   - Biostatistics or study design
   - Patient advocacy or lived experience
   - Other (please say)
5. **Which of the six diseases do you know well?** (checkboxes)
   - A20 haploinsufficiency (TNFAIP3)
   - SSADH deficiency (ALDH5A1)
   - Tangier disease (ABCA1)
   - Leukocyte adhesion deficiency I (ITGB2)
   - Alexander disease (GFAP)
   - Danon disease (LAMP2)
   - None of them, but I can help with methods
6. **How would you like to contribute?** (checkboxes; the dashboard's "What we need from you" section describes each)
   - Audit rater: rate 250 extracted values against their quoted evidence on a blinded web page (10 to 15 hours)
   - Tie-breaker for the audit (a few hours)
   - Adjudicator: review candidate variants from their dossiers (10 to 15 minutes per candidate)
   - Cohort reader: fill a comparator sheet from a natural-history paper (1 to 2 hours per sheet)
   - Methods or domain reviewer: read the paper and tell us what is wrong, missing or unclear (1 to 3 hours)
   - Advise on ClinVar or OMIM submissions
   - Something else (please say below)
7. **Roughly how many hours could you give over the next eight weeks?** (multiple choice: under 2; 2 to 5; 5 to 10; 10 to 20; more than 20)
8. **Weeks you are away between now and the end of January** (short answer)
9. **What did you read or watch?** (checkboxes: the video; the short paper; the deck; the full report; the candidate list)
10. **What is the most important weakness you see in the study so far?** (paragraph)
11. **What would make the results more useful to your field or your patients?** (paragraph)
12. **Questions for the team** (paragraph)
13. **May we contact you at the email above about the next steps?** (yes / no)

## Settings to switch on

- Collect email addresses (verified).
- Limit to one response per person: off (people may want to add thoughts later).
- Send respondents a copy of their answers: on.
- Responses to a linked Google Sheet, so they can be exported for the adjudication and audit scheduling.
